How it works

So simple

no sign up, no payment, nothing
1
Click "Open the Free Journal"

The journal opens right in your browser — on your phone or laptop. No download, no sign up needed.

2
Write your entry

Go through the 5 pages at your own pace. Tap or click your moods, fill in your thoughts, rate your café.

3
It saves on your device

Everything you write saves automatically on your own device. Come back anytime — it's all still there.

4
Print it if you want

Hit the print button inside the journal to get a physical copy of your entry to keep, stick in a scrapbook, or journal alongside.
